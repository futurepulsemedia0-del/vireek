## Method and limits

- Dead-code detection follows static `import`/`export … from`/`import()` only. Strings, `import.meta.glob` (directories excluded automatically) and runtime lookups are invisible: verify before deleting.
- "Large" is a proxy for change risk, not proof of bad design. Thresholds: >1,500 lines listed individually.
- Duplicate-library entries list only packages that differ by major version; transitive duplicates are usually harmless.
