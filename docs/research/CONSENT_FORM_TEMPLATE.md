## 1. Why we are asking

We want to find out where the Vireek mobile app for technicians is hard to use — for example with work gloves, in bright sun, in a noisy truck, or with weak phone signal — so we can fix it. We are testing the **app**, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

## 2. What happens

- One session of about `[30–45]` minutes, plus a short conversation.
- You will do a few everyday tasks in the app (for example open a job, add a note, check truck stock) on a **practice account with made-up jobs**. No real customer or job is involved.
- Some tasks will be done under different conditions (gloves, outdoors, in a parked vehicle, with signal turned on and off).
- A researcher will watch and take notes. We will ask you to say what you are thinking.

## 3. Your safety

- You will never be asked to use the app while driving, climbing, or working on live or dangerous equipment. The vehicle will be parked.
- You may stop at any moment, for any reason, without explaining.
- Tell us if you feel unsafe, uncomfortable or distracted from a hazard. We will stop.

## 4. What we collect

We collect only what we need: a code (such as "P07") instead of your name, your trade, your experience range, your phone model, the gloves you used, what you did in the app, how long tasks took, and what you said.
We will **not** record the screen, audio or video unless you tick the separate boxes in section 8.
We do not collect real customer data in this study.

## 5. Your employer

If your employer or a customer of Vireek helped us find you, **they will not be told whether you took part, what you did, or what you said.** Only combined, anonymous results (for example "several technicians struggled with X") may be shared. Taking part or not taking part will not affect your job, pay, or your employer's relationship with us. `[COUNSEL: confirm wording is accurate for the recruitment route actually used]`
