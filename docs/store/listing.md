### Description (max 4000)
```text
Vireek Field is the companion app for technicians whose company uses Vireek. Open it in the morning and see today's jobs, then capture everything customers and dispatch need without leaving the job site.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- See your jobs for today and open the brief for each one
- Take job photos with the camera and attach them to the job
- Record voice notes instead of typing on a small screen
- Scan equipment barcodes or tap an NFC tag to identify equipment
- Keep working without signal: captures are saved on your phone and sync automatically when you are back online
- Share your trip with dispatch while you are on a job, so the office can give customers accurate arrival times
- Get push notifications when a job is assigned or changes
- Protect customer data with an optional Face ID, fingerprint or passcode lock

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
Large touch targets, a fast Today view and offline-first capture, so a basement with no signal never costs you a photo.

PRIVACY
Location is used only to stamp job photos and, while you are working on a job, to share your trip with dispatch. You can delete your account and data from Settings → Security at any time.

Vireek Field requires a Vireek account from your company. Ask your administrator to invite you.
```
