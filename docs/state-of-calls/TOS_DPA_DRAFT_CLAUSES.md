# DRAFT clauses for counsel (not final, not legal advice)

## Terms of Service — "Aggregate statistics programme (optional)"
1. **Optional.** Contribution is voluntary, off by default, and not a condition of any Vireek service. Only an account owner may opt in.
2. **What we use.** If you opt in, Vireek derives counts and rates from your account data (e.g. numbers of calls, missed, emergency and booked calls by month, language counts, lead response time, completed-job amounts, trade and US Census region). We do not use call recordings, transcripts, names, contact details, addresses or any individual record.
3. **How it is published.** Statistics are combined with those of other contributing businesses and published only where at least ten businesses, and sufficient volume, stand behind a figure, no single business dominates it, and values are rounded. We do not publish, and will not attempt to derive, information identifying you or your customers.
4. **Licence.** You grant Vireek a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to publish the resulting aggregate statistics. They do not identify you.
5. **Withdrawal.** You may withdraw at any time in Settings. Withdrawal takes effect for all editions built after you withdraw. Aggregates already published are not recalculated. Closing your account has the same effect.
6. **Benefits.** Contributors may see a comparison against published figures for similar businesses.
