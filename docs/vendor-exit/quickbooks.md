## 5. Exit steps

1. Declare the trigger (vendor insolvency, price/terms change, security incident, regulatory block, quality drop) and record the decision and date.
2. Freeze new dependency: feature-flag new onboarding to the vendor.
3. Run the export in section 2 and verify row counts and checksums against Vireek tables; store the export in a private bucket.
4. Build or enable the replacement behind the seam in section 1; shadow-run before any traffic moves.
5. Cut over progressively; keep rollback available until the vendor contract ends.
6. Notify affected tenants, keep an export path, then remove the connector.
7. Provider-specific: Notify tenants and keep CSV export available.
