## 2. Data export method

A cloned voice model is generally NOT exportable [VERIFY]. Plan on re-cloning at the new vendor from the original consented samples (if still retained) or asking each speaker again.

## 3. Alternatives

Cartesia, PlayHT, Resemble, Azure or Google TTS (stock voices only).

## 4. Estimated switch effort

**M** — ≈ 2–4 person-weeks plus re-consent review [VERIFY]. Scale: S < 2 person-weeks · M 2–5 · L 5–10 · XL > 10 (one engineer, including tests and rollout; excludes vendor lead times, contract negotiation and tenant coordination). The measured coupling tier above is an input, not the estimate: it counts import reachability, not difficulty.

## 5. Exit steps

1. Declare the trigger (vendor insolvency, price/terms change, security incident, regulatory block, quality drop) and record the decision and date.
2. Freeze new dependency: feature-flag new onboarding to the vendor.
3. Run the export in section 2 and verify row counts and checksums against Vireek tables; store the export in a private bucket.
4. Build or enable the replacement behind the seam in section 1; shadow-run before any traffic moves.
5. Cut over progressively; keep rollback available until the vendor contract ends.
6. Revoke vendor credentials, request deletion of vendor-held data and written confirmation [COUNSEL], and update the public sub-processor list and customer notice (`scripts/compliance/gen-subprocessors-by-region.mjs --check`) [COUNSEL].
7. Provider-specific: Voice samples are biometric-adjacent: re-using them with a new vendor needs a consent-scope check and a deletion at ElevenLabs [COUNSEL] (see `docs/compliance/transfers-and-contracts.md` row D9).
