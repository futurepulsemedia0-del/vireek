## 2. Data export method

Customers, subscriptions, invoices and charges export through Dashboard/API reports [VERIFY]. Card data can only move through a vendor-assisted PCI-to-PCI transfer on request [VERIFY]; raw card numbers are not in Vireek. Connected (tenant) accounts cannot simply be moved to another platform [VERIFY]: expect each tenant to re-onboard.

## 3. Alternatives

Adyen, Braintree/PayPal, Checkout.com, Square, Mollie (EU). Platform/marketplace payouts need a Connect-like product at the candidate.

## 4. Estimated switch effort

**XL** — ≈ 12–24 person-weeks; Connect KYC re-onboarding dominates [VERIFY]. Scale: S < 2 person-weeks · M 2–5 · L 5–10 · XL > 10 (one engineer, including tests and rollout; excludes vendor lead times, contract negotiation and tenant coordination). The measured coupling tier above is an input, not the estimate: it counts import reachability, not difficulty.

## 5. Exit steps

1. Declare the trigger (vendor insolvency, price/terms change, security incident, regulatory block, quality drop) and record the decision and date.
2. Freeze new dependency: feature-flag new onboarding to the vendor.
3. Run the export in section 2 and verify row counts and checksums against Vireek tables; store the export in a private bucket.
4. Build or enable the replacement behind the seam in section 1; shadow-run before any traffic moves.
5. Cut over progressively; keep rollback available until the vendor contract ends.
6. Revoke vendor credentials, request deletion of vendor-held data and written confirmation [COUNSEL], and update the public sub-processor list and customer notice (`scripts/compliance/gen-subprocessors-by-region.mjs --check`) [COUNSEL].
7. Provider-specific: Dual-run: new customers on the new processor while existing subscriptions run off.
8. Provider-specific: Dispute evidence (`dispute-evidence`) and results-plan metering have processor-specific semantics.
9. Provider-specific: PCI scope and card-token portability [COUNSEL].
