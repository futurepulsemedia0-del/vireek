# Vireek usability test script (5 owners, 45 min each)

**Who:** 5 owners of 1–10 person plumbing / HVAC / electrical businesses who have never seen Vireek. Mix of phone-first and laptop-first. No employees of ours, no friends.
**Rules:** Say nothing about how the product works. Read each task aloud, then be quiet. Ask them to think aloud. Record screen + audio with consent. Never help before the time limit; note every hint you give as a failure. **Do not fabricate or round results.**

## Setup (before each session)
- Fresh test account (no data), a real phone the participant can answer, a stopwatch.
- Participant's trade selected during Task 1, nothing else pre-configured.

## Tasks

| # | Task (read aloud) | Success | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You just signed up. Get this to answer your business calls." | Finishes the setup wizard and reaches the Launch step with services, hours and a phone number set, with no hint | 10 min |
| 2 | "Check that it really works. Call it and see what it wrote down." | Starts the test call from the wizard (or Getting Started), answers it, and can say what was captured | 4 min |
| 3 | "A customer called while you were out. Find out who, and what they wanted." | Opens Today or Call History and names the caller and the request | 2 min |
| 4 | "Change the price of a service you offer." | Finds Price Book and saves a changed price | 3 min |
| 5 | "You want to stop the assistant from taking calls tonight." | Finds the right setting and states what will happen to calls; or explicitly cannot (record as failure) | 3 min |

Score each task: **Pass** (no hint, within limit) / **Pass with difficulty** (finished, over limit or hesitated > 20 s on one screen) / **Fail** (hint needed or gave up). Record: time, wrong clicks, words the participant used for things (their vocabulary, verbatim), any term they did not understand.

## Questions (after tasks, in order)
1. In your own words, what does Vireek do for your business?
2. What was the most confusing moment? What did you expect to happen?
3. Was anything on the menu you didn't understand? Which?
4. Would you let this answer your real customers tomorrow? What would stop you?
5. What would you tell a friend in your trade about it, in one sentence?
6. (0–10) How easy was it to get started? Why that number?

## Decide after 5 sessions
- A task fails for ≥ 2 of 5 owners → fix before any new feature.
- Any term ≥ 2 owners did not understand → rename it (use their word).
- Median time to Launch (Task 1) is the number to compare with our funnel dashboard (/staff/activation, median minutes from signup to "Assistant configured"). Report both; do not average across different participants.
