# Selection criteria

Goal: a board that represents the customers Vireek wants to serve, not just the friendliest ones. All numbers below are proposals **[VERIFY]**.

## 1. Eligibility (all required)
- Active paying Vireek customer for at least `<6>` months, in good standing.
- Business owner, GM, ops or office lead with authority over how the business works.
- Willing to sign the confidentiality terms **[COUNSEL]** and the conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- Uses at least `<2>` core Vireek areas (e.g. calls, dispatch, quoting, billing) so feedback is grounded.

## 2. Scoring (0–3 each; invite top scores subject to §3)
| Criterion | 0 | 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Candor — gives specific, critical feedback | silent / only praise | volunteers concrete problems |
| Representativeness — fits a target segment | outlier | core segment |
| Usage depth — real, recent usage | dormant | daily across modules |
| Availability — can attend and read pre-reads | rarely | reliably |
| Peer standing — respected, collaborative | disruptive | constructive |
| Strategic insight — sees beyond own pain | self-only | patterns across the trade |

Evidence for each score is written down (support history, usage data, a short conversation) — no scores from impressions alone.

## 3. Balance (so no one voice dominates)
Seats are allocated across: trade (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, other), company size band, region/market, tenure (new vs. long-term), and at least one critical-but-fair account. No single customer segment above `<40>`% of seats; no competitor of another member in the same seat group without disclosure (see conflict rules).

## 4. Exclusions
Customers in a billing dispute or legal dispute with Vireek (decide case by case), people employed by or advising a direct Vireek competitor, Vireek employees and their close relatives, and anyone who asks for membership in exchange for a commercial concession.

## 5. Process
1. Build a long list from usage and support data (aggregate; respect each customer's privacy settings).
2. Score with two Vireek reviewers independently; resolve differences by discussion.
3. Check balance (§3) and conflicts ([conflict-of-interest-rules.md](conflict-of-interest-rules.md)).
4. Invite personally; explain commitments, confidentiality and that there are no guaranteed benefits.
5. Record who was considered, who was invited and why in the selection record (kept internally; retention **[COUNSEL]**).
6. Keep a waitlist; rotate seats at term end.

## 6. Fairness
Selection reasons must be job-related and non-discriminatory. Do not use protected characteristics in scoring **[COUNSEL]**.
