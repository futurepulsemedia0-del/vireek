# CAB charter

Status: draft v0.1 — owner: `<name>` — review: every 12 months. Legal wording **[COUNSEL]**.

## 1. Purpose
Give Vireek structured, honest input from home-service business owners and operators so product decisions reflect real field operations. The board advises; it does not decide.

## 2. What the board does
- Reacts to roadmap themes, prototypes and pricing/packaging *concepts*.
- Reports what is and is not working in their business, including negative feedback.
- Tests ideas against real workflows (dispatch, quoting, billing, calls).

## 3. What the board does not do
- Make product, pricing or hiring decisions, or hold any governance, equity or voting rights.
- Receive guaranteed features, discounts or priority support because of membership. Any commercial benefit is a separate written agreement **[COUNSEL]**.
- Speak for Vireek, or be quoted publicly without separate written consent.

## 4. Structure (proposal — adjust **[VERIFY]**)
- Size: `<8–12>` member companies, one primary representative each, plus one Vireek executive sponsor and one facilitator (non-voting).
- Term: `<12>` months, renewable once by mutual agreement; staggered so not all seats turn over together.
- Seat mix per [selection-criteria.md](selection-criteria.md).

## 5. Member commitments
Attend `<75>`% of sessions, read pre-reads, give candid feedback within the agreed window, follow [conflict-of-interest-rules.md](conflict-of-interest-rules.md) and the confidentiality terms.

## 6. Vireek commitments
- Share pre-reads at least `<5>` business days ahead.
- Log every piece of feedback and publish a decision (accepted / planned / declined with reason / parked) within `<30>` days — see [feedback-to-roadmap-log-template.md](feedback-to-roadmap-log-template.md).
- Never present early-access or roadmap items as commitments. Roadmap statements are non-binding unless in a signed agreement **[COUNSEL]**.
- Close the loop: report back at the next session what shipped because of their input — only what is true.

## 7. Confidentiality and data
Members may see unreleased material under a signed NDA **[COUNSEL]**. Vireek does not share one member's data or feedback with another member without that member's consent. Personal data of board members is limited to name, company, role, contact details, and is handled per Vireek's privacy policy **[COUNSEL]**.

## 8. Compensation
None by default. If any is offered (gift, credit, honorarium), it follows the conflict-of-interest rules, is recorded in the register, and its legality in the member's jurisdiction is checked **[COUNSEL]**.

## 9. Changes, removal, ending
Charter changes: executive sponsor approves after informing members. A member may leave at any time; Vireek may end a membership for repeated absence, confidentiality breach or an undisclosed conflict, with notice and a stated reason. The board may be paused or dissolved by Vireek with `<30>` days' notice.

## 10. Success measures (set before launch, review at 6 and 12 months)
Share of feedback items with a recorded decision on time; number of roadmap decisions changed by board input (counted from the log, not estimated); member attendance; member-reported usefulness (short survey). No targets are set in this charter **[VERIFY]**.
