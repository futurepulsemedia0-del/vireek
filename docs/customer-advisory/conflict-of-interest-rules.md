# Conflict-of-interest rules

These rules protect the members, the board's credibility and Vireek. Wording is a starting point only **[COUNSEL]**.

## 1. What counts as a conflict
A member or Vireek participant has a conflict when a personal, financial or business relationship could reasonably be seen as affecting advice or decisions. Examples:
- Owns, works for, advises or invests in a Vireek competitor or a vendor/partner Vireek is evaluating.
- Is negotiating pricing, a contract, a dispute or a refund with Vireek.
- Has a family or close personal relationship with a Vireek employee involved in the board.
- Would gain directly from a specific roadmap outcome (e.g. is a paid integration partner).
- Competes for customers with another member in the same market.

## 2. Disclosure
- Written disclosure before joining and at each annual review; verbal check at every session ([agenda](agenda-template.md)).
- Disclosures are recorded in a register kept by `<role>`; access is limited to that role and the executive sponsor.
- New conflicts must be disclosed within `<10>` business days of becoming aware.

## 3. Recusal
- A conflicted member may attend but does not give input on the affected topic, and leaves the room/call for it when the facilitator asks.
- Vireek staff with a conflict take no part in selecting that member or deciding that member's feedback items.
- Recusals are noted in the session notes.

## 4. Competitors and shared sessions
Members who compete locally are told in advance. No member is asked to reveal pricing, customer lists, margins or other competitively sensitive information; facilitators stop such discussion. Whether competitor-related participation raises antitrust concerns is a legal question **[COUNSEL]**.

## 5. Confidentiality
- Unreleased Vireek information is confidential under the signed terms **[COUNSEL]**.
- Vireek does not disclose a member's feedback or business data to other members without that member's consent.
- Membership is not consent to be named, quoted or used as a testimonial. Public use requires separate written, dated consent, consistent with the existing no-unverified-claims rule for customer stories.

## 6. Gifts, hospitality, compensation
- No cash or items of more than nominal value in either direction. Nominal limit: `<amount>` **[VERIFY]**.
- Anything of value (including credits or discounts) is recorded in the register; none may be tied to feedback content or to a favourable opinion.
- Rules differ by country and for public-sector or regulated customers **[COUNSEL]**.

## 7. Using board input
Vireek uses feedback to inform decisions but does not owe members credit or compensation for ideas unless agreed in writing. Handling of ideas and intellectual property **[COUNSEL]**.

## 8. Breaches
Report to `<role>`. The executive sponsor reviews, the member can respond, and outcomes range from a reminder to removal. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern is prohibited.

## 9. Review
Rules reviewed yearly and after any breach.
