# Meeting cadence

Proposal — adjust after the first two cycles **[VERIFY]**.

## Rhythm
| Event | Frequency | Format | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full board session | Quarterly | Video (in person once a year if budget allows) | 90 min |
| Working group (one theme, 3–5 members) | As needed, max monthly | Video | 45 min |
| Async pulse (3 questions) | Between sessions | Form / email | 5 min |
| Annual review | Yearly | Video | 60 min |

## Timeline for each quarterly session
- **T–21 days:** pick themes from the roadmap log and open questions; confirm date (agree dates for the whole year at kickoff).
- **T–10 days:** send invitation, agenda, and pre-read (≤ 2 pages per topic; mark confidential material).
- **T–5 days:** pre-read deadline for Vireek; members send questions or data they want covered.
- **T–1 day:** run-through with facilitator and note-taker; confirm demos work on a non-production environment with no real customer data.
- **Session day:** follow [agenda-template.md](agenda-template.md).
- **T+2 days:** thank-you note and draft notes to members for correction.
- **T+5 days:** every feedback item entered in the log ([template](feedback-to-roadmap-log-template.md)).
- **T+30 days:** decision recorded for each item; send a short "you said / we did" summary.

## Roles
Facilitator (runs time, balances airtime), executive sponsor (owns decisions), product lead (presents), note-taker, log owner (keeps the feedback log current).

## Ground rules
Candor over politeness; one conversation at a time; no selling in the session; confidentiality applies to everything unreleased; missing a session means sending written input and reading the notes.

## Attendance
Two missed sessions in a row triggers a check-in, not automatic removal. Quorum for a session: `<half>` of members **[VERIFY]**.

## Recording
Sessions are recorded only with every attendee's agreement; otherwise notes only. Storage and retention of recordings **[COUNSEL]**.
