## Facilitation prompts
- "Tell us about the last time this happened in your business."
- "What would you stop using if we shipped this badly?"
- "Who else in your company would object?"
- Avoid: "Would you use this?" (invites polite yes). Ask what they do today and what it costs.

## Do not
Promise dates or features; ask members to vote on roadmap; collect other members' or customers' personal data; show real customer data in demos.

## After the session
Complete [feedback-to-roadmap-log-template.md](feedback-to-roadmap-log-template.md) within 5 business days. Send notes for correction within 2 business days.
