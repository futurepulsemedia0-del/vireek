Symptom: calls are not reaching the AI receptionist or are routed wrongly. Check Dashboard > Phone Numbers (/dashboard/phone-numbers) for the numbers on the account and Dashboard > Call Routing (/dashboard/call-routing) for hours, holiday, voicemail and overflow rules. When to escalate: if the number shows as connected but live calls still do not arrive, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) with the number and a time of a test call.
