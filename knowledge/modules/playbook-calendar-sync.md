Symptom: booked appointments do not appear on your calendar. Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 / Outlook are live integrations. Connect or reconnect them from Dashboard > Integrations (/dashboard/integrations); booking rules live in Dashboard > Booking settings (/dashboard/booking-settings) and the schedule itself in Dashboard > Calendar (/dashboard/calendar). Disconnecting and connecting again from Integrations is the first thing to try. When to escalate: if reconnecting does not fix it, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support).
