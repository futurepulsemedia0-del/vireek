Symptom: callers reach voicemail or nobody answers. Check in this order: (1) Dashboard > Phone Numbers (/dashboard/phone-numbers) that the number is active; (2) Dashboard > Call Routing (/dashboard/call-routing) that hours, holiday and overflow rules are not sending calls elsewhere; (3) that your plan still has included minutes, because usage beyond included minutes is billed per minute at your plan's overage rate rather than switched off. When to escalate: if all three look right and live calls still fail, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) with a test-call time, or use live chat during support hours.
