Symptom: appointment confirmations or missed-call text-backs are not being delivered. Open Dashboard > Settings > SMS compliance (/dashboard/settings/sms-compliance) to review the messaging registration and compliance settings for the account. When to escalate: if the page shows an unresolved registration problem, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support).
