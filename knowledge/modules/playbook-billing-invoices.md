Symptom: you need an invoice, want to change plan, or want to update the card on file. Where to go (signed in): Dashboard > Billing (/dashboard/billing). Plans and upgrades/downgrades are under Billing > Plans (/dashboard/billing/plans); invoices are under Billing > Invoices (/dashboard/billing/invoices); the payment method is under Billing > Update payment (/dashboard/billing/update-payment). Plan changes take effect on the next billing cycle and the difference is prorated. When to escalate: a charge you do not recognize, a double charge or a refund request cannot be resolved by the public assistant; open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) or use live chat.
