Symptom: you are not receiving call summaries or alerts. Review Dashboard > Notifications (/dashboard/notifications) for which alerts are on and where they are sent, and check your spam folder for email summaries. When to escalate: if a channel is enabled and test alerts still do not arrive, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support).
