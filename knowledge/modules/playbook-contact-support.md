Ways to reach the Vireek team: live chat from the assistant during support hours (Mon-Fri, 9am-6pm ET); a support ticket for signed-in customers at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support); the contact form at /contact; or email ali@vireek.com (usually a same-day reply). Paying customers get support by plan: standard 24-hour response on Starter, priority 4-hour on Professional, a dedicated Slack channel on Business, and a named account manager with an SLA on Enterprise. The AI assistant itself is available 24/7. The phone number shown on the site connects to the live AI voice demo, not a human support line.
