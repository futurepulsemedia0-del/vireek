Symptom: you want to bring data in or take it out. Import contacts and records from Dashboard > Data Imports (/dashboard/imports). Export reports and records from Dashboard > Exports (/dashboard/exports). When to escalate: formats that are rejected, or a request for a full account data export that these pages do not cover, go to a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) or ali@vireek.com.
