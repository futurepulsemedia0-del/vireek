Symptom: QuickBooks data is missing or out of date. QuickBooks is a live integration. Reconnect it from Dashboard > Integrations (/dashboard/integrations) and review the accounting connection under Dashboard > Accounting (/dashboard/accounting). When to escalate: if a reconnect still fails or entries are duplicated, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) and include the approximate date of the affected entries.
