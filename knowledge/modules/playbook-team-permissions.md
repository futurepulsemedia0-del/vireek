Symptom: a teammate cannot see something or you need to add someone. Manage people from Dashboard > Team (/dashboard/team). Seat limits depend on the plan (Free 1, Starter 2, Professional 5, Business 15, Enterprise unlimited). When to escalate: if someone has the right role but still cannot open a page, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support).
