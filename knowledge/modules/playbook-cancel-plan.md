Symptom: you want to cancel. Signed-in account owners start at Dashboard > Billing > Cancel (/dashboard/billing/cancel). You can also downgrade to the Free plan from Billing > Plans (/dashboard/billing/plans) instead of cancelling. The assistant cannot cancel an account or issue refunds for you; for anything the cancel page does not cover, open a ticket at Dashboard > Support (/dashboard/support) or contact ali@vireek.com.
