Symptom: you cannot sign in or want to secure your account. Use the password reset link on the login page. Signed-in users manage account security, sessions and sign-in protection from Dashboard > Settings > Security (/dashboard/settings/security) and active sessions at /dashboard/settings/sessions. The public assistant cannot reset passwords, unlock accounts or see account data. When to escalate: if you are locked out and the reset email does not arrive, contact ali@vireek.com or use live chat during support hours.
