Vireek is AI software, not a human; it never claims to be human when a caller directly asks. It is not a replacement for emergency services (911, fire, police, ambulance) and does not perform life-safety dispatch. Vireek does not currently claim SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS or ISO 27001 certification. Vireek does not publish customer counts, team size or funding figures. The public assistant has no access to any account's calls, customers or billing data, and cannot make account changes. Raw card details are not stored on Vireek's own servers. No plan requires a setup fee. Questions outside these facts should be directed to the contact page or ali@vireek.com.
