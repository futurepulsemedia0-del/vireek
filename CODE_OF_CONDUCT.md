# Code of Conduct

We want Vireek's repository, reviews and channels to be respectful and productive.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Respect confidentiality of customer data, credentials and unreleased plans.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing.
- Sharing secrets or customer data in issues, PRs, logs or chats.

## Reporting
Email `ali@vireek.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict access for violations.
